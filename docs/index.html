The designs shown are precomputed by the inverse model: every nearby candidate is built, the ones that fail the geometric admissibility screen are discarded, the porosity and surface area of the survivors are measured exactly, and the closest to the request is returned. The page runs entirely in the browser with no server. Meshes offered here are decimated for the web; the blend weights and level-set offset in each entry regenerate the cell at full resolution through the pipeline in this repository.
